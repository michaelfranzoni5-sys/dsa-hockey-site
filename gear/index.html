How These Work Together

The real magic happens when you combine tools. Here's how to blend them into game-speed training:

🏒 Balance Ball + Green Biscuit

Stickhandle on an unstable surface. If you can control the puck here, you'll handle it through any check. 3 sets of 60 seconds.

🎯 Slide Board → Shooting Tiles

Build explosive lateral speed on the slide board, then step off and fire. Train your legs to generate power sideways — then transfer it into your shot. 10 reps each side.

💪 Bands + Top-Hand Tool

Stride against resistance while working top-hand control. Trains your nervous system to keep hands soft while legs fire — elite-level coordination.

🥏 Caufield Tutor + 100 Pucks

Rapid-fire shooting at specific zones. Hit low blocker 25 times, then high glove 25 times. Don't leave until you hit each zone clean.
