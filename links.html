Toe Drag & Soft Chip

Toe drag the puck into your feet, then soft chip over a stick. Advanced drill — master the basics first.

✅ Do's
  • Start with a toe drag, bringing the puck toward your feet
  • Keep your feet wide — maintain your base
  • Use your top hand to lead and let the bottom hand slide
❌ Don'ts
  • Don't try this before mastering basic stickhandling first
  • Don't stand too straight or narrow — you lose your base
  • Don't worry about mistakes — it's training, not a game
